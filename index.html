📘 Aturan Was & Were

Was dan Were adalah bentuk lampau dari kata kerja to be (am, is, are).

💖 WAS dipakai untuk:

I He She It

I was happy yesterday. Saya senang kemarin.
She was in Bali last week. Dia (perempuan) berada di Bali minggu lalu.

💚 WERE dipakai untuk:

You We They

You were at the park yesterday. Kamu berada di taman kemarin.
They were tired after school. Mereka lelah setelah sekolah.

📍 Was/Were + Tempat

Kita bisa menceritakan di mana kita berada di masa lalu.

I was at home last night. Saya di rumah tadi malam.
We were in the library yesterday. Kami di perpustakaan kemarin.
He was at the beach last Sunday. Dia di pantai hari Minggu lalu.

😊 Was/Were + Perasaan

Kita juga bisa menceritakan perasaan di masa lalu.

I was hungry this morning. Saya lapar pagi ini.
She was sleepy in class. Dia mengantuk di kelas.
They were excited about the trip. Mereka bersemangat tentang perjalanan.

❓ Bentuk Tanya & Negatif

Was she happy yesterday?
Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't. Apakah dia senang kemarin?
Were you at school last Monday?
Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. Apakah kamu di sekolah Senin lalu?
They weren't tired.
He wasn't at home. Mereka tidak lelah. / Dia tidak di rumah.
